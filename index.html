04

Pauli–Gibbs attention

A Pauli-structured energy view of self-attention — scores parameterized as Gibbs distributions over Pauli feature energies, with O(T) bilinear forms and head-level specialization into kernel and directional modes, studied on a TinyLM testbed.

以 Pauli 结构的能量视角理解自注意力：将打分参数化为 Pauli 特征能量上的 Gibbs 分布，实现 O(T) 双线性形式与注意力头的核 / 方向分工。

06

Pseudospectral solver for 3D Gross–Pitaevskii

FFT-based Laplacians take the 3D Gross–Pitaevskii equation from O(N6) to O(N3 log N); correctness validated through conservation laws and symmetry analysis, with the framework generalizing to reaction–diffusion systems.

基于 FFT 的拉普拉斯算子将三维 Gross–Pitaevskii 方程的复杂度从 O(N6) 降至 O(N3 log N)，以守恒律与对称性验证，可推广至反应扩散系统。
